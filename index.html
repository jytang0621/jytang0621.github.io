Jingyu Tang

I am currently a final-year undergraduate student at Huazhong University of Science and Technology and a research intern at Microsoft Research Asia, advised by Jianxun Lian. Starting from Fall 2026, I will start my PhD journey at UT Austin, advised by Prof. Jiaxin Pei.

My research interests are generally around evaluating and improving the capabilities of AI for human-AI collaboration. More recently, I am working on benchmarking and training proactive MLLMs to better assist and collaborate with humans.

I’m always enthusiastic about collaborating with researchers from diverse fields. If you’re interested in working together, feel free to reach out to me!
